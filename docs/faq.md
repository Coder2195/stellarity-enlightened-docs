# FAQ

## What versions are supported?
As of the time of this writing, only the latest version of Minecraft will be supported, with Fabric being the sole modloader.

## Why not 1.20.1 or 1.21.1 or Neoforge?
Maintaining a mod takes work. It is difficult to maintain such a big mod for multiple versions. You are free to fork and see how difficult it is to maintain one version alone, and if you do it well enough, you are permitted to host your 1.20.1/1.21.1/Neoforge fork of the mod alongside mine.
